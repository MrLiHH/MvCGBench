# Research access

1. Complete the [Tencent Docs application form](https://docs.qq.com/form/page/DUEdpTENpQkdBZ0xa).
2. Provide your name, institution, institutional email, research purpose, and acceptance of the data-use requirements.
3. Applications are reviewed manually. Approval is not automatic.
4. Approved applicants receive a cloud-drive download link by email, valid for 30 days. Please check your spam folder.

Access covers feature files and accompanying annotations for the remaining 90 movies. Original movies, extracted images, and soundtracks are not provided. Packages are still being prepared; application availability does not indicate that downloads are already ready.

Do not forward download links or redistribute restricted data. Use the data only for the approved research purpose, respect third-party rights, and do not attempt to reconstruct original audiovisual media. Access may be revoked for violations. Link expiration does not automatically delete previously downloaded files.

Application responses and applicant contact information are not published in this repository.
